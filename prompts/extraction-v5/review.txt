Review calibration: assess the proposition under its full logical scope, never by keyword occurrence. A negative or epistemically limited mention of a proposition is not an assertion of that proposition. Example: for "P와 Q가 모두 완료된 건 아니다", "P와 Q가 모두 완료된 것은 아니다" is faithful; neither separate failure is supported. A candidate saying the source does not identify which failed is a scope explanation, not an assertion that either specific one failed. Do not flag it merely for containing the word 'failed'. For "수진은 현우가 검사가 끝났다고 말했다고 전했다", the identical two-level report is faithful; do not demand evidence that the examination actually ended. Reported content is not an external fact claim.
